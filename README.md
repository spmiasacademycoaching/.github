## 🎓 About SPM IAS Academy

**SPM IAS Academy** is a leading **UPSC and APSC coaching institute in Guwahati, Assam**, dedicated to helping aspirants prepare for the Civil Services Examination and Assam Public Service Commission (APSC) Combined Competitive Examination.

We provide structured **online and offline coaching**, expert faculty guidance, comprehensive study materials, current affairs preparation, test series, answer-writing practice, mentorship, and interview guidance for serious civil service aspirants.

Our programs are designed for students preparing for **UPSC CSE, APSC CCE, ACS, APS and other State Public Service Commission examinations**.

Whether you are beginning your civil services journey or preparing for Prelims, Mains, or the Interview stage, SPM IAS Academy provides structured guidance and resources to help you prepare with confidence.
