## 🎓 About SPM IAS Academy

**SPM IAS Academy** is a leading **APSC and UPSC coaching institute in Guwahati, Assam**, dedicated to helping aspirants prepare for the **Assam Public Service Commission (APSC) Combined Competitive Examination (CCE)** and the **UPSC Civil Services Examination (CSE)**.

Whether you are starting your civil services preparation or preparing specifically for **Prelims, Mains or Interview**, SPM IAS Academy provides a structured learning environment focused on conceptual clarity, examination strategy and consistent practice.
